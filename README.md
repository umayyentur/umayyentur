I work at the intersection of **machine learning and medicine** — turning unstructured clinical text into structured, analysis-ready data, and building the full-stack tooling that puts it to work.

- 🔬 **Clinical NLP** — information extraction from Turkish medical text: diagnoses, treatments, biomarkers, temporal event sequences

- 🧠 **LLMs & transformers** — semantic search, embedding-based retrieval, and evaluating model reasoning on domain-specific tasks

- 📊 **Applied ML** — predictive modeling, survival analysis, time-series forecasting, and CNN-based medical image analysis

- 🛠 **Full-stack data products** — Python/Flask & Django backends, PostgreSQL, Docker; analytics dashboards that clinicians and researchers actually use

- 🎓 **Explainable AI** — SHAP/LIME and human-in-the-loop evaluation (MSc thesis on interpretable quality control in manufacturing)

- 🌱 **Currently exploring** — agentic workflows, retrieval-augmented reasoning, and mobile development with React Native

<h3 align="left">🧪 Selected Work</h3>

> These live in private repositories — happy to walk through the approach, architecture, or results on request.

**Clinical NLP & LLMs**

- **Trial eligibility matching from clinical notes** — a pipeline that reads free-text discharge summaries and surfaces the clinical studies a patient may be eligible for, combining structured rule-based filtering with semantic retrieval and language-model reasoning over eligibility criteria.

- **Document Q&A assistant** — upload a book, get a structured summary and ask questions about it. Retrieval-augmented pipeline over long documents with source-grounded answers.

**Medical imaging**

- **DermAI** — dermatological image analysis that classifies skin lesions from a photo, separating conditions that look alike to the naked eye (e.g. acne vs. fungal infection). Built as an early triage aid, not a substitute for a dermatologist.

**Computer vision**

- **Satellite change detection** — a model that compares before/after satellite imagery of the same area and localises what changed, for monitoring construction, damage, and land use over time.

- **Gemstone classification** — fine-grained image classification identifying gemstone type from a photograph across a visually similar class set.

**Document AI**

- **Receipt OCR & expense parser** — OCR pipeline that reads a photographed receipt and extracts structured line items, totals, and amounts paid in EUR, turning paper receipts into queryable expense data.
